# SpaceGod canon: human history, 2100 to 3026

This is SpaceGod's own universe. It is not Prose's GLMZ and shares nothing with it (US-T857). The game is set in
**3026**: a new game opens on 1 January 3026, nine centuries after the first permanent settlements off Earth.

Humanity in 3026 is a Kardashev Type I civilization, and only just. Across Sol and the systems beyond the gates it
commands on the order of 10^16 to 10^17 watts, about as much as falls on all of Earth. It is spreading outward faster
than it ever has. It is also at war with itself, and it has a pest in the dark between the stars that every world shoots
on sight and almost nobody worries about.

The Manalith (the Midnight Geode at Wendmere, in Nightglass) is **not canon**. It is an easter egg: it can be found and
looked at, and nothing in this history explains it, mentions it, or depends on it.

Where this document and the game disagree, the game's simulation wins for numbers (populations, fleets, prices), and
this document wins for story (names, dates, why things are the way they are). Names below that the game already uses
are marked in **bold** on first use.

## 2100-2160: The Long Recovery

The twenty-first century ended with Earth poorer, hotter and more crowded than it had hoped to be, but not broken. The
2100s were spent fixing what could be fixed: sea walls, drowned coasts rebuilt inland, carbon pulled back out of the
air, and the slow return of a world economy that had shrunk for forty years.

Space never stopped in those decades; it just got cheap and dull. Reusable heavy launchers flew every day. Orbital
industry (fibre, crystals, pharmaceuticals, the first solar power satellites beaming energy down) was a business, not
an adventure. A few thousand people lived off Earth at any one time, mostly in low orbit and at a handful of lunar
south-pole bases mining ice.

- **2118**: The Artemis-era polar bases merge into one permanently crewed settlement, Shackleton Station. Its first
  child is born in 2124.
- **2131**: The first commercial solar power satellite delivers a gigawatt to the ground. By 2160 orbit supplies 6% of
  Earth's electricity, and the habit of thinking of orbit as Earth's power plant never goes away.
- **2146**: The Orbital Accords. Earth's major powers agree a single registry and traffic authority for everything above
  100 km. It is the seed of everything the Federation later becomes.
- **2157**: The first humans walk on Mars to stay. The settlement, a few dozen people under ice at Arcadia Planitia, is
  abandoned twice in its first twenty years and never quite dies.

## 2160-2290: The Cislunar Age

- **2163-2191**: The **Ascension Tether**, the one space elevator, is raised from Isla Isabela in the Galápagos. Thirty
  years of work, three failures and a carbon-nanotube industry the size of a nation's go into it. When the first
  climber reaches geostationary orbit in 2191, the price of reaching space drops by a factor of twenty overnight.
  People call it **the Thread**. It is still running in 3026, run by the Federation's Tether Authority, and it is still
  the busiest single structure in human space.
- **2190s-2230s**: Earth orbit fills. Ring habitats, factories, depots and the first true cities in space, a few
  thousand people each, grow at geostationary orbit and the Lagrange points. Their union, **Earth Orbital**, is the
  oldest settlement in the game.
- **2204**: **Tycho City** is founded on the Moon, the first lunar settlement planned as a city rather than a base. Mass
  drivers on the Moon begin throwing ore and ice to Earth orbit; lunar material builds most of what is in orbit by 2240.
- **2238**: **Kepler Freight Lines** is chartered at Earth Orbital to run cargo between Earth, the Moon and the
  Lagrange stations. It is the oldest company still trading.
- **2251**: The fusion torch. The first practical fusion drive flies, and the inner solar system stops being measured in
  years and starts being measured in months.
- **2266**: **Ares Prime** is founded on Mars, at the old Arcadia site, as a planned colony of 10,000. It reaches that
  in eleven years.
- **2280s**: The first permanent stations at Venus (**Aphrodite Aerostat**, floating in the clouds) and Mercury
  (**Caloris Works**, a mining and solar town in the great basin).

## 2290-2420: The Expansion

Fusion opened the whole system, and for a century humanity went everywhere it could.

- **2296**: **Ceres Hub** is founded. Ceres becomes the crossroads of the asteroid belt: ice, metal, and a port for
  everything going outward.
- **2310s**: The Belt rush. Thousands of claims are staked on asteroids in a few years. Most fail; the ones that
  survive become the Belt's founding families. **Vesta Ore and Haulage** is founded at Ceres in 2319.
- **2341**: Jupiter. Settlements on Ganymede and Callisto, sheltered from the radiation, mine ice and helium-3.
  Ganymede's towns federate as the **Ganymede Union** in 2362; **Jovian Bulk Carriers** is founded there in 2368.
- **2370s**: **Stickney Depot** on Phobos becomes the Martian fuel port. Mars's population passes a million.
- **2390s**: Saturn's moons. Titan's hydrocarbon seas are worth more than any ore: the **Kraken Port** settlement on
  Titan's Kraken Mare is founded in 2394 to pump them, and **Kraken Hydrocarbon Company** in 2401.
- **2400s**: Earth's population stabilises near nine billion. More people now live off Earth than lived on it in 1800.

The Orbital Accords authority, renamed the Sol Authority in 2302, governs all of it from Earth. It governs the inner
worlds well and the outer ones badly. Laws are written at Earth for Earth's interests, taxes flow sunward, and the Belt
and the moons are hours of light-lag away from any say in what happens to them.

## 2420-2560: The Charter Wars

- **2423**: The Ceres Strike. Belt miners refuse to ship ore sunward until the Sol Authority gives the Belt seats in its
  assembly. The Authority sends ships. Nobody fires, and nobody backs down.
- **2431**: The **Belt Consortium** is declared at Ceres Hub: the Belt's settlements and companies, governing
  themselves under a charter of their own. Earth refuses to recognise it.
- **2431-2449**: The First Charter War. Mostly a war of blockades, seized cargo and sabotage across distances so large
  that a battle could take a month to approach. It ends in the Treaty of Vesta: the Consortium is recognised as an
  equal, Earth keeps the inner system.
- **2452**: The Sol Authority, on Earth, the Moon and Mars, reorganises as the **Sol Federation**. Mars is given full
  membership in exchange for staying.
- **2470s-2510s**: Jupiter's and Saturn's settlements watch the Consortium win and want the same. In 2489 Titan,
  Ganymede's breakaway towns, Callisto and the scattered settlements of the outer system sign the charter of the
  **Outer Reach Compact** at Kraken Port.
- **2503-2531**: The Second Charter War, the Federation against the Compact. Far bloodier than the first: the first
  true fleet actions in human history are fought near Saturn, and Callisto's largest town is destroyed in 2518. The
  war ends in exhaustion rather than victory. The Ganymede Union joins neither side; it later aligns with the
  Consortium.
- **2530s-2560**: The three powers settle into the shape they still have in 3026: the Federation holding Earth, the
  Moon, Mars and the inner system; the Consortium the Belt and Jupiter's trade; the Compact Saturn and beyond. The
  Federation and the Compact have never trusted each other since.

The Independents (the free traders, free towns and unaligned ships who answer to nobody's navy) date from these wars
too: crews who refused to take sides, flew under their own colours and were left alone because everyone needed them to
carry cargo. **Meridian Free Traders**, founded at Tycho City in 2526, is the oldest of their companies; **Long Haul
Carrying Company**, founded at Ares Prime in 2611, the largest.

## 2560-2700: The Lightless Crossing

- **2566**: The first interstellar ship leaves: *Lantern*, a sleeper ship of 2,000 for Alpha Centauri, at a tenth of
  the speed of light. It arrives in 2611.
- **2580s-2650s**: Eleven more ships follow to the nearest stars. Three are never heard from again.
- **2614**: The cruise drive. A field drive that pushes a ship along at many times what any torch could manage, though
  still far below light speed. Mars is a day from Earth; Saturn is a week. The solar system becomes one economy for the
  first time, and Ares Haulage (founded 2622) gets rich carrying it.
- **2640s**: Alpha Centauri's first settlement, around Proxima, is founded by *Lantern*'s children. Messages home take
  four years and change each way.
- **2670s**: Radio from the colonies, years old when it arrives, is a cultural fixture across Sol. People listen to
  news of births and deaths at Centauri that happened before their own children were born. Some of it is still on the
  air in 3026, still travelling outward.

## 2700-2810: The Gate Century

- **2709**: The first jump gate is opened, between Earth's L1 point and a sister gate carried by sleeper ship to Proxima
  over the previous forty years. A ship crosses four light-years in an afternoon. Nothing in human history, not fire,
  not flight, changes things as fast.
- **2710s-2780s**: Gates are carried, or built in place, to every star within about twenty light-years. Hyperlanes, the
  routes between them, become the map of human space. Every gate is a fortress, because every gate is the only way in.
- **2734**: First contact, of a kind. A survey ship at Barnard's Star finds a drifting mass of flesh, bone and hair, a
  hundred metres across, warm, and breathing. It is alive, it is not from anywhere humans have been, and when the survey
  ship's boat touches it, it eats the boat. People call it **the Biomass**.
- **2735-2760**: The Biomass turns up everywhere at once, or seems to: globules drifting in from the dark between the
  stars into every system with a gate. They drift, they grow on what they eat (wrecks, debris, hulls, stations), and
  they creep toward gates as if they know what gates are for. A globule that reaches a gate goes through.
- **2748**: The Gate Protocol, the one agreement every power has kept since. Every gate is ringed with guns and forts
  that fire on any globule on approach, and the far gate fires on anything that comes through. Every world, moon and
  station builds orbital guns. Every armed hull, whatever its flag, engages the Biomass on sight.
- **2751**: The Carolina Fall. A globule the size of a building gets past the defences at Earth and comes down in the
  hills of the old south-east United States. It is not stopped in time. By the time the Federation has the area walled
  and burning, it covers most of three old states. It has been walled, watched and burned ever since; it has never got
  out. The Exclusion, as everyone calls it, is visible from orbit, red as meat and breathing, and Earth has learned to
  live with it, as people live beside a volcano.
- **2760s-2810**: The Protocol works. Globules are burned as fast as they come. No system holds more than a handful at a
  time; none has grown past a few megatonnes. In fifty years the Biomass goes from terror to nuisance to background.
  Pilots joke about it. Corporations fly waste to the Exclusion's edge and drop it in, because it eats that too.

What the Biomass is, where it comes from, and why it seeks out gates are not known in 3026. Most people do not think
about it. The few who study it point out that it has never once stopped arriving, and that nobody has ever found where
it arrives from.

## 2810-2960: The Long Frontier

- **2810s-2900s**: The gates reach out. Each power builds its own lanes and holds its own frontier systems: the
  Federation spreading from Earth's gates, the Compact from Saturn's, the Consortium buying and building where the
  trade is best. Hundreds of colonies are founded, most small, a few becoming worlds in their own right.
- **2822**: The Frontier Charter. Any company that builds a gate may hold a colony charter at its far end for a century.
  A gold rush follows, and the great carrying companies grow to rival the powers that charter them.
- **2840s**: The **Black Banner**. Pirates have always existed; the gates made them an empire. The Black Banner starts
  as a confederation of raiders working the long lanes and grows into something like a nation of outlaws, with hidden
  havens in every system (Blackwater, on Deimos, has been one since 2861, and nobody has managed to keep it closed).
- **2870-2874**: The Gate War. The Federation and the Compact fight over the lanes beyond Saturn. It is short, it is
  fought at gates, and it teaches both sides that a gate can be held by a few forts against a fleet. Neither wins.
- **2900s**: Carriers. Navies stop building single great ships and start building carriers that launch a hundred
  fighters through a gate in minutes, because a gate fight is decided in its first few minutes. The fighter pilot
  becomes the hero of a thousand serials.
- **2930s**: The Earth Orbital ring passes twelve million people, the largest city in human space. Earth's surface,
  with the Exclusion fenced off in one corner of one continent, is mostly parks, farms and old cities, and a place
  people from the colonies visit as pilgrims.

## 2960-3026: The Threshold

- **2968**: The Kardashev line. Federation statisticians announce that humanity's total power use has passed 10^16
  watts: by the old measure, a Type I civilization. Each power claims the credit. Nobody's life changes the next day,
  but the number gets painted on the hull of every new ship for a decade.
- **2980s-3010s**: Spreading. More colonies are founded in these thirty years than in the century before. The frontier
  is thinly held: a gate, a fort, a town, and light-hours of dark.
- **3009**: The Compact refuses to renew the Frontier Charter for Federation companies in its systems. Federation
  companies refuse to leave.
- **3019**: The Federation and the Compact go to war for the third time. In 3026 the war is still on, fought at gates,
  in lanes and around the colonies of the frontier: no decisive battle, a lot of wrecks. The Consortium trades with
  both, and the Black Banner raids everyone.
- **3026**: The present. The game begins on 1 January.

## The Biomass, for writers

- It is a minor threat, and it looks manageable: ten globules at most in any system, burned on sight by everyone,
  never past a few megatonnes. That is how people in 3026 see it, and that is how the game plays it.
- It is never explained. It came from the dark between the stars in 2734 and keeps coming. Do not give it an origin, a
  mind or a plan in canon text. People may speculate in barks and news; canon does not answer.
- On Earth it is the Exclusion: one fenced, burning, breathing zone in the old south-east United States since 2751.
  Contained, watched, and a tourist sight from orbit.
- Its only behaviours are the game's: it drifts, eats wrecks and hulls, grows, buds, and creeps toward gates.

## Rules for new canon

- Dates in game text come from this file. A founding year, a war, a first flight: look here first, and add here when
  something new needs a date.
- Before 2100 is real history and needs no canon here. Real astronomy (stars, exoplanets, their discovery years) is
  real history too.
- Nothing from Prose's GLMZ, and nothing about the Manalith.
- Keep the Biomass small in the telling; see above.
